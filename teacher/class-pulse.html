Checkpoint target and task

What this pulse can and cannot say

  • Demo mode: all understanding labels are fictional, manually authored fixtures to test the interface.
  • Teacher Firebase read: displays backed-up checkpoint writing and availability, but does not infer understanding without an approved semantic evaluator.
  • Backups ≠ submissions: student answers synchronize as snapshots and may be drafts. Canvas submission happens elsewhere.
  • Checkpoint 1 ≠ Checkpoint 2: they assess different prompts and are never shown as pre/post improvement.
  • Privacy: the browser uses a verified teacher Google account and Firestore security rules. It does not send writing to external AI, local storage, or analytics.
  • Sections: old backups may have no section identifier. Class filters are presentation tools, not a security boundary.
  • Confidence: student-reported 1 Lost through 5 Could teach it; not a correctness measure.
  • Trends: the demo line reflects fictional response arrivals. Production needs actual comparable observations to show a learning trajectory.
