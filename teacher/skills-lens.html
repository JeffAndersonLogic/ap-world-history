Drag in the submissions.zip straight from Canvas, Assignment then Download Submissions. The Lens reads it here in this tab, so there is no Terminal step and nothing is uploaded anywhere. Add your class roster CSV alongside it and the Lens can also name the students who did not submit at all, which is the one thing the submissions folder cannot tell you.

Already unzipped it? Select the submission files themselves and drop those, all at once. responses.csv and exceptions.csv from scripts/parse-canvas-submissions.js still work too, together or separately, and so does a responses.json from --json.
