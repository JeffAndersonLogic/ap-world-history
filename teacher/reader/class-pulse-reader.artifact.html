PRIVACYWhat this page does with the writing

  • It sends Claude the question, the criteria, the rubric and one numbered answer at a time. No names, classes or accounts are in what Class Pulse copies.
  • It keeps nothing. Close or reload the page and the pasted writing is gone from it. Nothing is saved to this page or shared with anyone who opens it.
  • It runs in your own Claude for Teachers account, the service the district approved for this.
  • Its readings are formative suggestions. Nothing here is a grade.
