The Mongol Empire

How did the Mongols turn conquest into a system of rule, and how did that system reshape Afro-Eurasian exchange?
End TargetStudents can explain how Mongol conquest, governance, and the Pax Mongolica changed political control and exchange while recognizing that Silk Road trade already existed before Mongol rule.

Instructional Guardrails

Must / Should / Could

Teacher intelligence stays here. The projector stays cinematic.

Must

  • Separate conquest from governance.
  • Explain mobility, organization, intelligence, and siege adaptation.
  • Use the Yam, khanates, tolerance, and local officials as solutions to distance.
  • Define Pax Mongolica as changed conditions of exchange, not the start of trade.

Should

  • Let maps and evidence do visual work.
  • Use CCOT language: baseline, continuity, change, why.
  • Keep returning to the paradox: destructive and connective.

Could

  • Extend with William of Rubruck.
  • Use BeInTheRoom as the application task.
  • Assign the full student lesson modules for reinforcement.
