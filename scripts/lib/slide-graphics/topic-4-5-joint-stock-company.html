Rulers' Tool 2 · A new kind of company

The Joint-Stock Company

Topic 4.5
1450 to 1750
Tool 1 shaped Tool 2

Mercantilism said: win trade and keep it at home. Rulers and merchants built a company to do both.

Financeexploration

Rulers and merchants raised the money for long voyages: the English East India Company (chartered 1600) and the Dutch East India Company, the VOC (1602).

Charterfrom the ruler

The charter gave one company the only right, in its own country, to trade with Asia.

Competeagainst rival rulers

Each state sent its company to win trade from the others. The VOC could even build forts, keep soldiers and fight wars.

Continuity and Change · Old

Merchants had pooled money for voyages for centuries.

Continuity and Change · New

A company with a government's monopoly, its own soldiers and the right to make war, sent by one state to compete with another.
