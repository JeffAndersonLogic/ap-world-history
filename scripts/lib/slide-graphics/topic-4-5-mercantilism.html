Mercantilism

Topic 4.5
1450 to 1750
The Belief

A kingdom grows strong by bringing in more gold and silver than flows out, so it must sell more to other countries than it buys from them.

Expandtheir economies

Sell more abroad than you buy, so gold and silver flow in and stay at home.

Controltheir economies

Spain ran trade with its American colonies through one office in Seville, the Casa de Contratación (1503). England's Navigation Act (1651) let goods from Asia, Africa and the Americas in on English ships only.

Claimoverseas territories

Take colonies that send raw goods home, buy the home country's goods and trade with no one else.

The name came later

Nobody called themselves a mercantilist. Later critics named these shared ideas; Adam Smith wrote of the “mercantile system” in 1776.

Two purposes of a colony
  1. To provide cheap raw materials
  2. To serve as a market for the home country's goods
