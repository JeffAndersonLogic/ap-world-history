BeInTheRoom · Topic 3.4 · Comparison

The Imperial Influence Council

It is c. 1700. A ruler wants to understand how the great land empires increased their influence. You are the historical adviser. Do not rank the empires. Compare the same method across two cases and explain how the method helped each state expand, consolidate power, command resources, or project legitimacy.

Your briefing

Four empires, shared governing problems

Ottoman Empire

Expansion: gunpowder infantry and artillery

Administration: devshirme and Janissary service

Revenue: tax farming and provincial systems

Legitimation: Sunni leadership and monumental architecture

Mughal Empire

Expansion: gunpowder armies and cavalry

Administration: mansabdar ranking and elite incorporation

Revenue: zamindar tax collection

Legitimation: court culture, mosques, mausolea, and varied religious policies

Safavid Empire

Expansion: military conquest on the Persian plateau

Administration: court and provincial elites

Rivalry: Ottoman and Mughal frontier competition

Legitimation: Twelver Shia state identity and royal patronage

Qing Empire

Expansion: Banner forces and frontier campaigns

Administration: Manchu institutions alongside Chinese bureaucracy

Revenue: established agrarian tax systems

Legitimation: imperial portraiture, Confucian rulership, and multiethnic patronage

Comparison matrix

Choose one method and keep the category constant

Military expansion
How did weapons, military organization, or frontier campaigns increase territorial reach?
Administration and elites
How did rulers recruit, rank, or incorporate officials and military professionals to consolidate authority?
Revenue
How did taxation, tribute, or assigned revenue provide the resources needed to sustain state power?
Legitimation
How did religion, art, architecture, or court culture make imperial authority visible and credible?

Council memorandum

Answer the Topic 3.4 question

Required move: choose TWO empires and ONE shared method. Identify a meaningful similarity or difference, use specific evidence from both, and explain HOW the method increased imperial influence. Finish by explaining why the pattern existed.
