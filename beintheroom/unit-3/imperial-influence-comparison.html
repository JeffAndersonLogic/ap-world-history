The Imperial Influence Council

It is c. 1700. A ruler wants to understand how the great land empires increased their influence. You are the historical adviser. Do not rank the empires. Compare the same method across two cases and explain how the method helped each state expand, consolidate power, command resources, or project legitimacy.

Your briefing

Four jobs, previously learned evidence

EXPAND

Ottoman: cannon at Constantinople

Mughal: artillery and firearms at Panipat

Question: how did military power increase territorial influence?

HOLD

Ottoman: devshirme service elite

Mughal: mansabdars in ranked imperial service

Question: how did servants extend central authority?

PAY

Ottoman: tax farming

Mughal: land revenue and zamindars

Question: how did revenue sustain state power?

JUSTIFY

Mughal: monumental architecture

Qing: imperial portraiture

Question: how did visible claims make authority credible?

Comparison matrix

Choose one job and keep the category constant

Expand
Compare military methods that helped defeat rivals or gain territory.
Hold
Compare servants or institutions that carried the ruler's authority.
Pay
Compare revenue methods that supported armies and government.
Justify
Compare religion, art, architecture, or court culture used to make authority credible.
