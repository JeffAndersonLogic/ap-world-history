BeInTheRoom · Topic 2.7 · Comparison

The Sultan's Trade Council

It is the mid-15th century. A ruler wants to understand the three great exchange systems that connected Afro-Eurasia. You are the historical adviser. Your job is not to name the “best” network. Your job is to compare like with like and explain why similar economic problems produced different solutions.

Your briefing

Three networks, one analytical matrix

Silk Roads

Environment: long overland corridors

Transport: caravans and caravanserais

Commercial systems: credit, banking, bills of exchange, paper money

Effects: Kashgar and Samarkand grew; luxury production expanded

Indian Ocean

Environment: seasonal monsoon winds

Transport: compass, astrolabe, larger ships

Commercial systems: ports and diasporic merchant relationships

Effects: Swahili states, Gujarat, and Malacca strengthened

Trans-Saharan

Environment: desert crossing

Transport: camel saddles and caravans

Commercial systems: caravan organization and state protection

Effects: Mali benefited from and facilitated expanding trade

Evidence packet

Compare function, not just vocabulary

Merchant risk
Every network had to reduce the danger, uncertainty, and transaction costs of long-distance exchange.
Geographic constraint
Road, ocean, and desert environments created different transportation problems and therefore different technological solutions.
Demand
Luxury demand connected distant consumers to producers; gold and salt created complementary demand across the Sahara.
Productive capacity
Expanding markets could change what societies produced, including textiles, porcelain, iron, and steel.
States and cities
Commercial nodes and states gained wealth and influence by protecting, taxing, servicing, or controlling exchange.
Diffusion
As networks widened, ideas, technologies, crops, and pathogens crossed regional boundaries more frequently.

Council memorandum

Write a balanced comparison

Required move: choose TWO networks and ONE shared category: environment, transportation, commercial practices/finance, demand/productive capacity, states/cities, or diffusion. Use evidence from both sides and explain WHY the similarity or difference existed.
