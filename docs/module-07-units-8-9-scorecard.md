The 13/14 scores reflect evidence pools that necessarily rely more heavily on clearly labeled epidemiological, economic, cultural, or cross-time reconstructions rather than mostly discrete primary artifacts. They still meet the authenticity standard because the reconstruction status is explicit and students are asked to reason about limits rather than mistake the card for a primary source.

The decisive late-course improvement is independence: students must select, reject, corroborate, complicate, qualify, and weigh evidence instead of completing a supplied sentence frame.
