# Landing Slides: AI Historical Cinematic Reconstruction Prompts

**Written 2026-10-22 at Jeff's request.** His standard of the same day: **every presentation's
last slide, the landing sentence slide, carries an AI-generated historical cinematic
reconstruction**, not words alone and not a real painting. It starts with Topic 3.3 (taught
2026-10-22) and runs through Topic 5.4. This replaces the landing half of
`docs/QUESTION-AND-LANDING-IMAGE-PROMPTS.md` for those topics; the question-slide prompts there
still stand.

Each prompt is written from the deck's own landing sentence and its approved story, never from a
new story. **Historical accuracy is written into every prompt as a requirement**, with the
period details spelled out and the usual mistakes for that scene named, so the generator is
told what correct looks like before it draws. A generator can still slip (a stray flag, letters
on a sign), so each prompt ends with a short "check before use" list: three things to look at,
about thirty seconds, before uploading.

The rules these pictures live under (`docs/PRESENTATION-AUTHORING.md`, "AI-generated images"):

- On the slide each one carries exactly `Historical Reconstruction - AI Generated`, which the
  slide library prints from `ai: true`. Ask for a clean file with no words in the picture.
- **Never in the Evidence Lab**, and never called evidence, slide notes included.
- A picture that fails a check is regenerated, not captioned around.

## How to use this page

1. Copy the whole prompt for the topic (everything inside the gray box).
2. Paste it into the image generator (written for Gemini; ChatGPT works too).
3. Look at the three "check before use" items. If one fails, regenerate.
4. Save the picture under the filename given and upload it to `assets/images/topics/X-Y/`, or
   hand it to Claude in a session and say "wire in the 3.3 landing picture."

fill in the braces from the deck's landing sentence and the topic's approved story.

```text
6. Show every people with dignity: no caricature, no exaggerated features, no costume stereotypes.

Must NOT appear: {the common mistakes for this scene}. Also no modern objects, and no text, letters, numbers, signs, logos or watermark anywhere in the picture.

COMPOSITION: 16:9 landscape. The main subject sits in the middle of the frame. {The bottom quarter / The top third} is a calm, plain area ({floor, water, ground or sky}) where a sentence will be placed over the picture. Keep the bottom-right corner empty.

STYLE: Photorealistic, like a still from a carefully researched historical documentary film. Light comes only from sources that existed then: sun, fire, torches, candles, oil lamps. Rich but believable color. Not a painting, not fantasy, not a video game.
```
