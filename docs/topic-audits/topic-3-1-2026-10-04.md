  the Ottomans and the Safavids". `lesson-3-1-empires-expand.js`.

## Needs Jeff's decision
- **The primary source is not clearly Tursun Beg's wording.** The intro calls it an "adapted
  passage" from Tursun Beg, but the text reads as a composed retelling (it blends the Caesar title
  with "Sovereign of the Two Seas and the Two Continents"). Recommend either replacing it with a
  real published translation, or relabeling it a classroom retelling of how Ottoman writers
  described the conquest. I could not check it against the original from here.
- **The lecture has a devshirme card and no rivalry card.** Card 3 is the devshirme system, which
  is Topic 3.2's, while KC-4.3.III.i (Kandahar, Tondibi) has no lecture card at all. Recommend
  replacing the devshirme card with a Kandahar and Tondibi card, written to the approved story.
- **The Map & Geography module shows only the Ottoman expansion GIF.** The new local map of the
  four empires, with Kandahar and Tondibi marked, fits the topic better. Recommend using it in
  the module, which changes what the module teaches.
- **The eBook chapter gives the Moroccan motive as the gold and salt trade.** The CED files the
  Songhai-Morocco conflict under political and religious disputes in Unit 3 and under economic
  disputes in Unit 4 (KC-4.3.III.ii). Recommend keeping the economic motive for Unit 4.
- **Facts to verify before a slide projects them:** how many times Kandahar changed hands
  (the chapter says four; the deck says "more than once"), the Tondibi force sizes, and that the
  Mughal emperors were Sunni Muslims (the deck and reading state it; the repo has no cited source).
