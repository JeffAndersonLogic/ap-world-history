# Topic 4.2 audit, 2026-10-12

Taught: Green 2026-10-30, Silver 2026-11-02. Neither room has had 4.2.

This audit is the Phase 9 accuracy loop of the 4.2 build: three independent review passes,
six reviewers in all, plus the four judgment reports. Every finding and its disposition is
in `docs/TOPIC-4-2-CLAIM-LEDGER.md` under "Review findings"; this record is the summary.

## Fixed
- The topic's central claim: "only a ruler could pay" was false (Henry VII licensed Cabot "at
  their own proper costs"; Portugal leased exploration to Fernão Gomes in 1469). Every surface
  now says rulers backed voyages, by paying for them or by licensing them.
- KC-4.1.III.A's cause, Portuguese ships and navigation skills, was on no required surface.
  Added to the First & 10, lecture card 2, the deck timeline, Learning Target 2 and SC2.
- Myths the old pages taught, all corrected: 1453 closing the spice road (now the qualified
  version, cited to Lybyer 1915), the Sagres school, Magellan circumnavigating (Elcano
  finished), da Gama's "sixty times the cost," Tordesillas "brokered by the pope."
- Ownership drift: conquest, smallpox, encomienda, the Columbian Exchange, Manila galleons,
  privateering and joint-stock companies cut to bridge sentences.
- Map: rebuilt to draw the English, French and Dutch routes; labels, ovals and arrows fixed;
  the teacher projector no longer crops it; the page key is numbered to match and says why
  the northern states went north.
- Checkpoints: Checkpoint 1 is the role of states; Checkpoint 2 shows the targets it assesses,
  asks what the state gave, and declares Causation; prompts rewritten for a ninth grader.
- Skill Builder moved to 5.B Making Connections, the CED's suggested skill.
- Evidence Lab: captions checked against the pictures (the da Gama image is a later engraving;
  the Malindi pillar dated 1498 or 1499 and moved since); `?width=1200` on every Commons URL;
  `evidenceLab.items`, never rendered, removed; fallback artwork built for 4.2.
- Deep reading and eBook chapter: Tordesillas, the Pacific, the circumnavigation, Cabot's
  landfall, the spice margin, Portuguese secrecy and readability corrected.
- BeInTheRoom (Santa Fe Bargain): CED objectives, plain roles, the Canaries, a hindsight card.
- Deck: the staircase replaced with a true-scale timeline; two slides that collided in the
  brand fonts fixed (caught by `slide-templates.browser.test.js`).

## Reviewed and left alone
- Absolutes report: "Every one of these failed at its stated objective" and "they never found
  one" (no northern passage was sailed until 1878 and 1903); "most of the men who sailed never
  came home" (about 90 of about 270 returned). Kept. "The one thing Europeans could offer" was
  narrowed to "the main thing."
- Skill alignment: Checkpoint 2 reads as Causation. Congruence: nothing flagged. Evidence
  authenticity: 4 of 4 cards carry something to examine.
- The Waldseemüller map is also 4.1's Evidence Lab card; here it is asked a 4.2 question (how
  fast news of the voyages spread), so it stays.
- The deep reading is above ninth grade in places; it is optional, labeled as such, and its
  intro and section 01 were rewritten plainly.
