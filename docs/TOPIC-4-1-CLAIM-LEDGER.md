p. 78) says what 4.1 must teach. Whether a sentence is true is settled by scholarly and
institutional sources, recorded here with what they actually say. A web summary that cites a
specialist study counts as a pointer to that study, not as the study; where only a pointer was
reached, the row says so and the claim stays narrow.

Status is exactly one of VERIFIED, NARROWED, REMOVED, NEEDS JEFF. A row marked **re-check
before projection** is supported at the story stage and gets a second, specialist source in
Phase 9 because it is a date, a number or easy to mythologize.
