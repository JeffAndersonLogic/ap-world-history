# Module 07 Evidence Lab Audit — Units 3-4

Audit standard: `docs/module-07-scaffolding-standard.md`.

An A grade requires at least **12/14** on the seven-part rubric, with no category scored zero:
authenticity, coverage, inference, claim connection, choice/comparison, scaffolding, and module distinctiveness.

## Important structural finding

The Unit-topic renderer builds Module 07 cards from `lesson.images`. Several Unit 3-4 data files contained thoughtful `evidenceLab.items` prose, but those item banks were not rendered as the live Evidence Lab gallery. In other words, the underlying content ideas were often much better than what students actually received.

The initial grade below evaluates the **live student experience**, not merely the quality of an unused item bank.

## Refinement principles applied

- Converted Unit 3-4 Module 07s into active `lesson.images` evidence galleries so the renderer shows the evidence students are meant to analyze.
- Preserved progressive release: Units 3-4 still cue observation and source limits, but students make more of the selection, category, and claim decisions than in Units 1-2.
- Added provenance and chronology labels for contemporary sources, later memory, and secondary reconstructions.
- Reduced “caption gives away the answer” language.
- Required same-category evidence when comparison is the historical skill.
- Added corroboration/limitation moves without duplicating full HIPP analysis from Module 08.
- Expanded regional and imperial coverage instead of letting one familiar case stand in for a whole topic.
