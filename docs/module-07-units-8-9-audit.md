# Module 07 Evidence Lab Audit — Units 8–9

## Scope

This pass audits every **active** Module 07 Evidence Lab in Units 8 and 9 against `docs/module-07-scaffolding-standard.md`.

- Unit 8: Topics **8.1–8.8** = 8 active Evidence Labs.
- Topic **8.9** is intentionally excluded because it is a documented six-module capstone variant. Its custom synthesis path does not expose the standard Module 07 surface.
- Unit 9: Topics **9.1–9.9** = 9 active Evidence Labs.
- Total active labs audited: **17**.

## Audit standard

Each lab is judged on the seven two-point categories in the permanent Module 07 standard: authenticity, coverage, inference, claim connection, choice/comparison, scaffolding, and module distinctiveness. An A requires at least 12/14 with no zero.

For Units 8–9 the progression target is late-course AP independence. Students should not be walked through a sentence frame. They should select evidence, decide relevance, make an inference, connect it to a defensible claim, identify limitations, corroborate or complicate across cards, and reject evidence that does not fit.

## Initial diagnosis

The existing lessons contained usable historical material, but the Module 07 experience was uneven for three systemic reasons:

1. The shared renderer displays Module 07 evidence from `lesson.images`, so a short image set could become the entire live evidence pool even when richer lesson data existed elsewhere.
2. Several late-course labs offered too few evidence choices for meaningful corroboration, complication, rejection, or qualification.
3. Captions and prompts sometimes did too much interpretive work for students rather than requiring them to decide what a piece of evidence could prove and what it could not prove.

The fix was not to turn Module 07 into another content lecture or another HIPP exercise. It was to make every active lab a compact AP-style evidence pool.
