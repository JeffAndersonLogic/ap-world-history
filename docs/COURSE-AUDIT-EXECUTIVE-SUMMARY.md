# BeHistorical AP World — Course Audit Executive Summary

**Governing source:** AP World History: Modern Course and Exam Description, Effective Fall 2026  
**Reference implementation:** Unit 2 — Networks of Exchange  
**Scope:** AP Units 1–9  
**Audit state:** Course-wide structural triage complete; CED-grounded deep audit complete for Units 1 and 3–9; Unit 2 retained as the certified control.

## Bottom line

The course is not broadly broken. Most lesson architecture, links, source/generated relationships, and runtime CED plumbing are healthy. The central quality risk is **instructional drift inside otherwise polished topics**: a lesson can contain the correct CED metadata while its targets, story, or checkpoints quietly assess an adjacent topic.

That pattern is concentrated in Units 3–5 and in the generic late-course template used by parts of Unit 9.

## What the automated scan found — and what it could not

The calibrated structural scanner reviewed **71 AP topics**:
- **61 CLEAN** on automated structural checks
- **10 REVIEW**
- **0 automated REPAIR**
- **0 automated REBUILD**

That result is useful but deliberately limited.

The deep audit then found real instructional mismatches inside several structurally CLEAN topics. That is the key quality-control lesson from this project:

> **Structural CLEAN does not mean CED/instructional certified.**

Automation is best used to find broken wiring, missing surfaces, KC-code drift, and source/generated problems. CED-grounded human review must still verify the actual topic story and what students are asked to demonstrate.

## Audit-process calibration

The audit process itself was tested before being trusted course-wide.

1. The first structural scanner produced false positives against the known-good Unit 2 reference.
2. Those rules were rejected and recalibrated.
3. Unit 2 was made a required control: all seven topics must return CLEAN before the structural scanner is accepted.
4. Outside calibration topics 3.2 and 8.4 were used to ensure the framework could handle different topic shapes.
5. A separate lexical “smart” checkpoint-matching experiment failed the Unit 2 control and was deleted rather than weakened until it passed.

The retained process is therefore intentionally conservative:

**automated structural triage -> official-CED deep review -> repair -> human instructional review -> locked contracts**
