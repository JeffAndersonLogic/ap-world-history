A Routine for Any Source: C.L.U.E.

Use it on any source you meet, whether a paragraph, a map, or a quotation. It takes about thirty seconds and saves you from the most common wrong turn, which is answering from a familiar word instead of the source.

C

Context

Place the source first: region, century, state, or network. Do this before you judge any detail.

L

Locate

Underline the exact words, map labels, institutions, or relationships doing the historical work.

U

Unpack

Ask how the evidence produces an outcome: revenue, legitimacy, labor, movement, hierarchy, or diffusion.

E

Evaluate

Test each choice against time, place, and mechanism. A familiar term can still be in the wrong place.
