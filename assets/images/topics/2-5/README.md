# Topic 2.5 Visual Upgrade Targets

The Topic 2.5 Teaching OS is functional now with stable repo-local and Wikimedia Commons fallbacks. For maximum classroom visual impact, replace the fallback visuals only after these curated assets are added here.

## Highest-value uploads

1. `2.5 - Afro-Eurasian Connectivity Hero.jpg`
   - 16:9 or wider.
   - A clean map/composite showing the Silk Roads, Indian Ocean, and trans-Saharan networks together.
   - Purpose: opening + map synthesis. Students should see one connected Afro-Eurasian system, not only the Silk Roads.

2. `2.5 - Cultural Diffusion Triptych.jpg`
   - Three strong panels: Buddhism in East Asia, Hindu/Buddhist influence in Southeast Asia, and Islam in sub-Saharan Africa or Asia.
   - Purpose: make the CED cultural-diffusion examples visible in one glance.

3. `2.5 - Paper and Gunpowder Transfer.jpg`
   - Artifact-first composition rather than labels on a generic map.
   - Purpose: show the two CED technological-diffusion examples as material technologies that moved through networks.

4. `2.5 - Urban Fortunes Diptych.jpg`
   - Left: thriving trade city/node (Samarkand, Kilwa, or comparable).
   - Right: urban disruption/decline (Baghdad 1258 manuscript or another defensible period example).
   - Purpose: visually teach the KC-3.3.II idea that city fortunes varied.

5. `2.5 - Travelers Triptych.jpg`
   - Ibn Battuta + Marco Polo + Margery Kempe.
   - Prefer a route/manuscript/source image for each rather than modern portraits.
   - Purpose: make the named CED traveler examples memorable without turning the slide into biography.

## Visual standard

- Prefer historically grounded maps, manuscript art, architecture, artifacts, and route diagrams.
- Avoid generic stock imagery and decorative portraits.
- Keep text-safe negative space where possible; the Teaching OS can then place copy without hiding the historical evidence.
- Repo-local assets are preferred for production stability.
- If an AI reconstruction is ever used, label it clearly as a historical reconstruction / AI generated.
