Reverse history · Semester elective

Every headline has a history. We run it backward.

Most history classes start at the beginning and walk forward to now. This one starts with a story that is happening today and traces it back, step by step, to the moment it actually began.

Six events, one repeatable method, and a story of your own that you follow all semester. Grades 9 to 12, twenty weeks, no prior history course required.

How a unit works

Five steps. Same five, every event.

You learn one method and then run it on story after story. By Event 03 you will not need the scaffolding, which is exactly when you take it outside to a story of your own.

01

The Brief

A CNN 10 clip and a short read from The Week. Today's pulse on the event, in the first ten minutes of the block.

Where in the World

The Trace

The spine of the course. Reverse-timeline cards that walk from today's fight back to the origin.

Evidence Lab

Primary sources from the roots, not the retellings. Read them, interpret them, weigh what they can and cannot prove.
