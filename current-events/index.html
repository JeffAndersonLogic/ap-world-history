Every headline has a history. We run it backward.

Most history classes start at the beginning and walk forward to now. This one starts with a story that is happening today and traces it back, step by step, to the moment it actually began. By the end you will know why the world looks the way it does, and you will be able to prove it.

The method

Reverse history, in four moves

  1. Start at NOWName one real event that is in the news this week.
  2. Ask what caused itNot what it resembles. What actually produced it.
  3. Keep going backFollow the causes until the trail leaves living memory.
  4. Land on the originFind the decision, invention, or accident that started it.

Five steps. Same five, every event.
