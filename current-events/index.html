The method

Reverse history, in four moves

  1. Start at NOWName one real event that is in the news this week.
  2. Ask what caused itNot what it resembles. What actually produced it.
  3. Keep going backFollow the causes until the trail leaves living memory.
  4. Land on the originFind the decision, invention, or accident that started it.

How a lesson works

Launch, trace, turn, argue.

You learn one method and then run it on story after story. The number of trace-back steps changes with the story, Lesson 1 has five of them, but the four movements never change. By the third lesson you will not need the scaffolding, which is exactly when you take it outside to a story of your own.

01

Launch

A clip, a short read, and one event you are standing inside. You are the primary source, so the lesson starts in your hand.

Now
02

The Trace

The spine of the course. One question, asked as many times as the story takes: okay, but what caused that?

Going backward
03

The Turn

Attack your own chain. A chain that feels tidy is not the same thing as a chain that is true, and this is where you find out which links hold.

Turning around
04

You Do

Your argument. A defensible claim, your strongest and weakest links, the smartest opposing view, and one honest concession.
